      How to play

      1. You are the whole crew. Walk around the Habitat and use its stations. Click a station's label and you'll walk there.
      2. Watch three meters. Power comes from solar panels, and only while the Sun is up. Life Support is your air and water. Radiation dose is how much harmful radiation your body has soaked up. It goes up a little all the time, and a lot in solar storms.
      3. Nothing is free. The antenna, grow lights, robot and Boost all use power. Moon dirt can buy shielding, repairs or extra oxygen, but not all three.
      4. Storms: keep the antenna on and Mission Control warns you early. Park the robot and hide in the Storm Shelter at the back. Every storm is sized from a real solar flare, and they change each time you play.
      5. Make it to sunrise with life support left and your mission goal met. Earn stars and research points to unlock new missions.

      Keys: WASD or arrows to walk, Shift to run, Space to jump, drag to look, E to use, F to speed up time, Esc to pause.

      Credits and NASA data

      Real NASA data in this game

        Game rates (power, air, dose) are made simple and sped up so a lunar night fits in a few minutes. The data cards after each mission give the real numbers. Each solar storm is sized from a real solar flare: the flare's class sets how strong the storm is and how long it lasts. The flares come live from NASA DONKI (the past 12 months) when your browser can reach it, otherwise from a list of real past flares. The game always says which one you got. Not every real flare sends a particle storm toward the Moon; in the game each one does.

        Made by

        Team BaseRace for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026: "Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer". 3D models are our own designs, built in Blender and inspired by real NASA hardware like the Foundation Surface Habitat and VSAT solar towers.

        We used Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to help write code, balance the game numbers, script the Blender models, check science facts and write text. Our team chose the design, directed the work and reviewed and tested the results.
