Moon Mission Planner

Three missions at the lunar south pole. Each one adds a new way for the base to get into trouble. Finish a mission, earn research points, and spend them in the lobby to open the next one.

Play a mission→ Earn research points→ Unlock and upgrade in the lobby→ Next mission opens

How the modes differ

Harder modes give you more to keep track of

The numbers only move a little between modes (crew use 25% less in Cadet and 10% more in Commander). Most of the extra challenge is attention: each step up hands the player more jobs.

Cadet · 3 things

  • Power and Oxygen meters
  • The mission goal (Water shows on Ice Hunters)

Helpers: robot parks diggers at night, battery forecast, 45-second storm warning, no broken panels. Crew can't come to harm; missing the goal still fails.

Explorer · 8 things

  • All four meters: Power, Oxygen, Water, Food
  • The mission goal
  • Parking diggers at night yourself
  • A regolith pile for the Oxygen maker
  • Storm warnings and broken panels

Helpers: battery forecast, 30-second storm warning. Crew may spend 30 seconds at an empty meter.

Commander · 10 things

  • Everything in Explorer
  • Moon dust
    Panels lose 4% of their power a minute (down to half) until tapped clean, 1 regolith each.
  • Pack the supply crate
    Same weight as Explorer's crate, but you split it between water, food and regolith.

No battery forecast, 15-second storm warning, 10 seconds allowed at an empty meter.

What makes a mission fail

Every mission adds new ways to fail

Players get the same build menu every time. What they don't get is enough regolith to build everything at once, so the order and amount they build decide how it goes. A mission ends as "not complete yet" when the crew spend too long with an empty meter, or when the mission goal is missed. The match always plays out to dawn first.

Lobby research lab

Research points

Points arrive after every mission, even an unfinished one, and are spent in the lobby. Unlocks add new modules and open missions; upgrades make the modules you already have better.

Earning points

First Night pays up to 20, 30 or 40 by mode, including stars. 1 star pays half, 2 stars three quarters, 3 stars the full amount. Ice Hunters pays 1.5x, Storm Watch 2x.

A typical Explorer path

  1. First Night, 2 stars: 23 points. Buy the Ice Drill (10). 13 left.
  2. Ice Hunters, 2 stars: 34 points, 47 total. Buy Regolith Shielding (20). 27 left.
  3. Storm Watch, 2 stars: 45 points, 72 total. Enough for three first-level upgrades, or the Water Recycler and two upgrades.

Any finished First Night opens Mission 2 straight away. Almost every pair of finishes opens Mission 3. The one exception is a Cadet with 1 star on both, who is 5 short; one replay closes the gap.
