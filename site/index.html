Your mission

You are the whole crew

Walk around the Habitat, use its stations, send a robot outside and go on moonwalks. Three meters tell you how the base is doing. Keep them healthy until sunrise.

Power

Solar panels only work while the Sun is up. Charge the battery by day and make it last through the lunar night.

Life Support

Your air and water. Run it on Eco to save power, or on Boost when you need air fast.

Radiation dose

It rises a little all the time and a lot in solar storms. Pile moon dirt on the Storm Shelter and hide inside.

Nothing is free. Everything that helps costs power, moon dirt, suit air or radiation dose. Choosing what to spend is the game.

Three missions

  1. 01

    First Night

    You just landed. Charge the battery while the Sun is up, then make it last through the dark.

    Goal: keep the Habitat running until the Sun comes back.

  2. 02

    Solar Storm

    The Sun is busy. Keep the antenna on for warnings from Earth and hide when the storm hits.

    Goal: keep your radiation dose low through two solar storms.

  3. 03

    Ice and Greens

    Food is low and the cargo lander is late. Send the robot for crater ice and water the greenhouse.

    Goal: grow enough food to have meals left at sunrise.

Play each mission as a Cadet, Explorer or Commander. Earn stars and research points to unlock the next one.

Inside the game

A look around the base

Real NASA data

The Moon in this game is the real one

Your base sits on Connecting Ridge, one of the Artemis III candidate landing regions NASA named in 2022. The ground, the sunlight and the storms all come from NASA.

The ground you walk on
Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter LOLA elevation model, 5 metres per pixel. NASA PGDA product 78
Sunlight, Earth view and permanent shadow
LOLA south pole illumination maps. They set your solar power, your comms gaps and where the ice is. NASA PGDA product 69
Solar storms
Real solar flares, live from the space weather database when your browser can reach it. NASA DONKI
Radiation limit
The crew health standard astronauts follow. NASA-STD-3001
Air and water numbers
Ewert and Stromgren (2019). NASA Technical Reports Server
Ice in craters
Water ice found in a permanently shadowed crater in 2009. NASA LCROSS

Game rates are made simple and sped up so a lunar night fits in a few minutes. The data card after each mission gives the real numbers.

How to play

Make it to sunrise

  1. Use the stations. Click a station's label in the Habitat and you'll walk there.
  2. Watch the three meters. Power, Life Support and Radiation dose.
  3. Spend with care. The antenna, grow lights, robot and Boost all use power. Moon dirt can buy shielding, repairs or extra oxygen, but not all three.
  4. Hide from storms. Keep the antenna on and Mission Control warns you early. Park the robot and go to the Storm Shelter.
  5. Reach sunrise with life support left and your mission goal met.

About

Made for Space Apps 2026

Team BaseRace built Moon Base Trainer for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 challenge "Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer". The 3D models are our own designs, made in Blender and inspired by real NASA hardware like the Foundation Surface Habitat and VSAT solar towers.

Source code on GitHub

Use of AI

We used Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to help write code, balance the game numbers, script the Blender models, check science facts and write text. Our team chose the design, directed the work and reviewed and tested the results.

Open-source tools

three.js (MIT), Rapier physics (Apache 2.0), Vite (MIT). Fonts: Fira Sans, Overpass and Fira Code (SIL Open Font License). Icons drawn in the style of Feather (MIT).
